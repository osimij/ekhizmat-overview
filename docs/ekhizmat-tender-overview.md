# eKhizmat — Platform Summary

eKhizmat is a unified digital public-services platform for citizens and businesses. It is comparable in purpose to Russia’s Gosuslugi: one online place where people can find government services, submit applications, book appointments, receive updates and track the progress of their requests.

The platform is designed to make access to public services simpler, faster and more transparent. Instead of visiting multiple offices or using separate websites, users can manage their interactions with government services through one clear, multilingual digital experience.

**In short: eKhizmat is a national-style digital gateway to public services, bringing government services closer to people through a single online platform.**
